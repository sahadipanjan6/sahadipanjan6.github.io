01 / About

Research with linguistic depth and engineering discipline.

My academic and professional journey spans research, higher education, and software engineering. I work on systems that connect human speech, language, emotion, and generative AI — with a particular interest in multilingual and low-resource settings.

Current themes include speech emotion recognition, expressive and multilingual speech generation, direct speech-to-speech translation, disfluency modeling, speech-text alignment, low-resource Indic language technologies, and robust speech representations.
